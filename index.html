Challenge

iad, one of Europe's largest networks of independent real estate agents, doesn't hire. It invites people to start their own business inside its network for a monthly fee. That is a harder sell than a job ad: a prospect has to understand the model, trust the numbers and picture their first months.

And the story had to work in four markets at once, each with its own offer, language and channels.

  • Explain the iad model on one page: a business of your own, not a job
  • Make the economics concrete: an agency's costs against one monthly fee
  • Keep one visual system across four markets, languages and formats

Design system

The Join iad page runs on a UI kit built on iad's brand guidelines and reviewed with iad International: Montserrat from Regular to Black, a digital palette around Official Blue and Coral, and components with states that developers and local teams can reuse.

  • A type scale from a 64 px Display to 14 px captions
  • A digital palette with W3C AA marks: Neutral 0–950, Blue 50–950 and Coral
  • Buttons, tabs, toggles, progress bar, accordion, fields and cards, each with states
